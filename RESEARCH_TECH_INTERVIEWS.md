# Tech Company Interview Research & Deduplication Matrix

This document summarizes our research across Reddit (`r/cscareerquestions`, `r/ExperiencedDevs`, `r/southafrica`, `r/johannesburg`, `r/capetown`), Glassdoor, Blind, and South African engineering hiring guides powering **Rehearse's** Company & Role Interview Intelligence Engine.

## Key Insight: Deduplicating Companies by Shared Interview Archetype

Most tech companies do not invent completely unique interviews from scratch. Across candidate reports globally and in South Africa, tech loops cluster cleanly into **11 distinct interview archetypes**. Grouping overlapping companies avoids wasting preparation time on redundant question banks while preserving company-specific nuances (like Amazon's Bar Raiser, Stripe's Bug Squash, Boxfusion's TestGorilla screen, or Deloitte's Assessment Centre).

## Expanded Tech & Adjacent Role Coverage (19 Roles)

- **Data Analyst / BI Intern:** SQL CTEs & window functions, Star Schema dimensional modeling, DAX KPIs, fraud/risk scoring, UAT, and executive storytelling.
- **Graduate Software Engineer / SWE Intern:** OOP pillars, design patterns, DAO/parameterized SQL, Git/testing, and live personal GitHub walkthroughs.
- **AI Developer / LLM Application Engineer:** Output validation & regex sanitization, graceful API retries, ONNX inference, Qdrant vector pipelines, and workflow automation.
- **Technology Consulting / Transformation Graduate:** Process mapping, requirement discovery, legacy digitization, and stakeholder buy-in.
- **Strategy, Payments & VCA Consulting Analyst:** Payments & card-portfolio cases, margin/liquidity math, cohort diagnostics, and executive recommendations.
- **Data Center Operations (DCO) Tech:** Server POST/BIOS/UEFI, DIMM/PSU/NVMe diagnostics, RAID, IPMI/BMC, Linux CLI logs, TCP/IP/DNS/DHCP, safety, and Amazon LPs.
